========================================================
1. THE PRODUCT
========================================================

1.1 What is NEXUS?

NEXUS is an AI leadership advisor. Think of it as having a
senior executive coach + talent strategist + market intelligence
analyst available on demand, powered by AI.

It's NOT a generic chatbot. It's NOT an HR tool. It's a premium
leadership intelligence platform for executives and organizations
that take talent seriously.

1.2 What can it actually do (today, not future)?

CORE: AI ADVISORY CHAT
- Conversational AI that gives executive-level advice on career,
  leadership, and talent questions
- Context-aware — remembers your profile, your assessments,
  your situation
- Not generic ChatGPT answers — grounded in LYC's leadership
  framework and data

CAPABILITY: LEADERSHIP ASSESSMENTS
- China Leadership Pipeline Diagnostic (flagship):
  5 dimensions, 6 leadership profiles, benchmarked against
  global executives across 47 markets
- SHIFT Suite assessments (5 more diagnostics): LEAP, QUEST,
  DRIVE, COACH, IMPACT — different lenses on leadership
- Each assessment generates a detailed narrative report with
  strengths, blind spots, and development recommendations
- Powered by LLM narrative generation + structured scoring

CAPABILITY: MATCH ANALYSIS
- AI-powered JD-CV matching
- Score candidates instantly against role requirements
- Breaks down fit by dimensions, not just a single number
- For both individuals (assessing role fit) and organizations
  (screening candidates at scale)

CAPABILITY: MARKET INTELLIGENCE
- Talent market mapping and competitive intelligence
- Track compensation benchmarks, talent movement,
  organizational structure across industries
- Data-driven insights on talent markets, primarily China/APAC

1.3 The product architecture (what's real):

LYC Intelligence (brand)
  └── NEXUS (the product — AI advisor)
        ├── Advisory Chat (primary interface)
        ├── China Leadership Pipeline Diagnostic
        ├── SHIFT Suite assessments (5 diagnostics)
        ├── Match Analysis
        ├── Market Intelligence
        └── Scorecard Builder
  Powered by DEX AI (the engine — attribution only)

Important: DEX AI is NOT a product. It's the engine. It appears
as "Powered by DEX AI" — like "Intel Inside" for leadership AI.

========================================================
2. WHO IS IT FOR?
========================================================

2.1 Primary audience — Individual leaders (B2C)
- Senior executives, high-potential leaders in China/APAC
- Typically Director level and above
- Age 35-55
- Ambitious, invest in their own development
- Skeptical of generic self-help, value data and rigor
- Willing to pay for premium tools and insights

Pain points:
- "I don't know where I stand in the market"
- "Career advice is either generic or expensive"
- "I don't have a clear picture of my leadership gaps"
- "Finding the right next role is guesswork"

2.2 Secondary audience — Organizations & search firms (B2B)
- Executive search firms (like LYC Partners)
- HR teams and talent departments
- Private equity portfolio companies
- Fast-growing tech companies building leadership teams

Pain points:
- "Candidate screening takes too long"
- "We don't have consistent scoring across recruiters"
- "We can't see the talent market clearly enough"
- "Our assessments are manual and subjective"

2.3 Tone & voice

We're premium, not pretentious.
- Sharp, confident language
- Data- and insight-driven
- No hype, no buzzword bingo
- Executive-level: we respect the reader's intelligence
- Warm but professional — not robotic, not overly casual
- We're on the leader's side, not selling from above

Brand voice: "We know leadership. We've built our careers on it.
Now we've built an AI that makes that expertise available to you."

NOT: "Revolutionary AI! Disrupting leadership! 10x your potential!"
(We hate that shit.)

3.2 Key design tokens (from live code)

- The 0px radius makes it feel precise and sharp — not cutesy,
  not startup-y

========================================================
PAGE 1 OF 3: NEXUS HERO PRODUCT PAGE (/nexus)
========================================================

This is THE page. This is where we send people.
This is what converts. Treat it as the flagship.

4.1 Purpose
Sell NEXUS. Make people want it. Explain what it is, what it does,
who it's for, why it matters. Get people to start — Executive
Introduction tier.

4.2 Primary conversion goal
"Start — Executive Introduction" → sign up / begin assessment

4.3 Secondary goals
- Browse to pricing
- Browse to B2B / enterprise
- Understand "Powered by DEX AI" credibility

4.4 Recommended page structure (you decide final, but these blocks should exist):

  SECTION 1 — HERO
  Big, bold, immediate.
  - Eyebrow label: "Leadership Intelligence" or "AI Leadership Advisor"
  - Headline: something that stops a leader mid-scroll.
    Current main landing has "Know where you stand. Know where to go."
    — that's good direction, make it about NEXUS specifically
  - Subhead: one sentence on what NEXUS is and who it's for
  - Primary CTA: "Start — Executive Introduction"
  - Secondary text/link: "See how it works" (anchor down)
  - "Powered by DEX AI" — small, subtle, below the fold of the hero

  SECTION 2 — WHAT NEXUS IS
  Short, clear product explanation.
  - 1-2 paragraphs of positioning
  - Why it's different from a chatbot, a course, a coach
  - Frame: "Your AI leadership advisor — always on, always sharp,
    built on years of executive search data"

  SECTION 3 — WHAT YOU CAN DO (capabilities section)
  Frame these as things you can DO with NEXUS, not separate products.
  4 capabilities:
  1. Get AI advisory — on career, leadership, talent questions
  2. Take leadership assessments — flagship: China Leadership
     Pipeline Diagnostic (5 dimensions, 6 profiles, global benchmark)
  3. Analyze role fit — Match Analysis: how do you stack up
     against any role description
  4. Access market intelligence — talent market data, compensation
     benchmarks, organizational insights

  Each capability: icon + title + 1-2 line description + link to
  go deeper (some link to pages that exist, some link to sections
  inside NEXUS — use your judgment on presentation)

  SECTION 4 — HOW IT WORKS
  3-step flow. Simple, clear:
  1. Start — create your profile, begin with an assessment or a
     conversation
  2. Discover — get personalized insights, benchmarked results,
     AI analysis
  3. Act — follow recommendations, track progress, keep moving

  SECTION 5 — FEATURED CAPABILITY SPOTLIGHT
  China Leadership Pipeline Diagnostic — this is our flagship
  assessment, the most concrete thing we have to show.
  - Name: China Leadership Pipeline Diagnostic (never "CPI")
  - What it is: comprehensive leadership assessment
  - What you get: 5 dimensions, 6 leadership profiles, benchmarked
    against 47 markets, full narrative report
  - CTA: "Take Complimentary Assessment" (never "free")
  - Visual: could be a mockup of the report, a results snapshot,
    or just strong design treatment

  SECTION 6 — FOR BUSINESS (B2B teaser)
  Acknowledge that some visitors are here for enterprise solutions.
  - Brief: "For teams, firms, and organizations"
  - 2-3 bullet points on enterprise value (candidate screening,
    pipeline assessment, team-level insights)
  - Link: "Explore enterprise solutions" → /b2b

  SECTION 7 — PRICING TEASER
  Show the 3 tiers briefly. Don't overload — just enough to make
  people click through to /pricing.
  - Executive Introduction (entry — complimentary access)
  - Executive Access (full individual — paid)
  - Enterprise (teams & organizations)
  - CTA: "See full pricing" → /pricing

  SECTION 8 — TRUST / CREDIBILITY
  Social proof or trust signals. What do we have?
  - "Powered by DEX AI" technology explanation (light)
  - "Backed by LYC Partners — 10+ years of executive search"
  - Benchmarked against 47 markets
  - Use whatever is true and credible — don't invent logos or
    testimonials we don't have

  SECTION 9 — FINAL CTA
  Big, clear, last push.
  - Headline: something punchy about starting
  - CTA: "Start — Executive Introduction"

  SECTION 10 — FOOTER
  Same footer as existing landing pages
  Links: NEXUS, For Business, Pricing, DEX AI, Legal, etc.

4.5 Design notes for /nexus
- This page should feel like the main event. It deserves the
  strongest design treatment.
- Make the China Leadership Pipeline Diagnostic feel substantial —
  it's our most concrete product and a great hook for new users.
- Don't clutter the hero. One clear message, one clear action.
- "Powered by DEX AI" should feel like a trust mark, not a product pitch.

4.15 Recommended structure:

  HERO
  - Headline: "Powered by DEX AI"
  - Subhead: "The intelligence engine behind NEXUS.
    Multi-agent AI systems, specialized assessment models,
    and talent market data — all working together."
  - Small note: "DEX AI is the engine. NEXUS is the product."
  - CTA: "Experience it in NEXUS" → /nexus

  Explain the system at a high level. Not too technical,
  not too vague.

    tasks (assessment, matching, analysis, advisory chat)
  - Assessment engines: structured scoring models + LLM narrative
    generation (DeepSeek-based)
  - Matching algorithms: multi-dimensional candidate-role fit
    with transparent scoring breakdowns
  - Market intelligence layer: structured talent market data
    from multiple sources
  - NEXUS orchestration: ties it all together in one
    conversational interface

  Visual: a simple architecture diagram or icon-based system map
  would be great. If you can design that, do. If not, clean
  text layout with strong typography works too.

  TRUST & SECURITY
  This section addresses the "should I trust this with my data?"
  question.
  - Data privacy
  - Secure infrastructure
  - Model quality and validation
  - LYC Partners expertise backing the framework
  (Be honest — don't invent certifications we don't have.
  Frame it as: serious technology, backed by a firm that's
  been doing executive assessment for over a decade.)

  BACK TO NEXUS CTA
  - Clear path back to the product
  - "See what DEX AI powers → NEXUS"

  FOOTER
  - Same footer components, teal-accented

========================================================

We're not in the generic AI chatbot space. We're not in the HR tech
space. We're in a niche we basically invented: AI-powered leadership
intelligence for executives and organizations that take talent seriously.

Who we get compared to:
- General AI chatbots (ChatGPT, Claude) — we're more specialized,
  deeper on leadership/talent, grounded in real assessment data

2. Not one tool — an integrated system of assessment + chat +

   we understand the China market deeply
4. Premium positioning — this isn't for everyone, and that's fine

Design vibe we want:
- Premium, editorial, precise
- Like a high-end consulting firm's digital product
- Not like a SaaS startup's landing page
- Not like a tech bro AI startup
- Confident, understated, substantial
