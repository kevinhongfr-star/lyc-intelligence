# DEX AI — Phase 3: Launch Strategy & Campaign Architecture
## Agency Comms Pitch Methodology — Phase 3
**Date:** 2026-08-07
**Status:** Draft v1 — Built on locked Phase 2. Launch framed as LYC Partners capability reveal, not a product launch. Discovery Principle drives the entire sequence — contradiction first, experience next, platform revealed over time.
**Tagline (locked):** Leadership, measured.

---

## Phase 3 Goal

Answer: **How do we actually roll this out — in what sequence, with what assets, over what timeframe?**

Important note: This is not a standard product launch. DEX AI is not a standalone product with a launch date, a waitlist, and a pricing reveal. It's a capability that's been built into every LYC Partners engagement. The "launch" is a gradual, layered reveal campaign where DEX AI is discovered through the quality of the work — not announced as a new thing.

**Launch model = Capability Reveal Campaign**, not Product Launch.

**Core principle:** The campaign itself follows the Discovery Curve. People encounter the contradictions, then the insights, then the stories, then they realize there's a system behind all of it. By the time DEX AI is explicitly named, they've already been sold on the outcome.

---

## 1. Launch Timeline — 12-Week Capability Reveal

12 weeks, four phases. Each phase deepens the reveal by one level. DEX AI visibility increases week by week, but never faster than the audience's natural curiosity.

| Phase | Weeks | Strategic Goal | DEX AI Visibility |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1: The Contradictions** | Weeks 1-3 | Establish the problem. Frame the tradeoffs. Build category authority. | Barely visible — mentioned in footnotes and asides |
| **Phase 2: The Craft** | Weeks 4-6 | Show how better search actually works. Build practitioner credibility. | Low to medium — "we've built systems for this" |
| **Phase 3: The Reveal** | Weeks 7-9 | Explicitly introduce DEX AI as the engine. Show it in context. | Medium — named, explained, but always in context of outcomes |
| **Phase 4: The Proof** | Weeks 10-12 | Client stories, data, case studies, measurable outcomes. | Medium to high — platform is visible but outcome still leads |

**Why 12 weeks:**
- Short enough to maintain momentum
- Long enough for the Discovery Curve to feel natural (not rushed)
- Aligns with a typical quarter of content output
- Each phase is 3 weeks — enough time to seed multiple ideas across all voices

## 2. Campaign Phases — Detailed Breakdown

### Phase 1: The Contradictions (Weeks 1-3)

**Strategic goal:** Establish that executive search is full of false tradeoffs. Position LYC Partners as the firm that understands these tensions better than anyone. Set up the "everyone accepts this as inevitable, but it doesn't have to be" frame.

**Key messages:**
- The search industry accepts tradeoffs that don't have to exist
- Speed vs. quality, relationship vs. data, judgment vs. data — these are false choices
- The most expensive hires aren't the fee — they're the 18 months after a bad decision
- Most hiring disagreements aren't about the candidate — they're about different people seeing different data

Nobody feels sold to. Everyone who reads this content thinks "these people get it." The contradictions are universally recognizable. DEX AI hasn't even been named yet, but the groundwork is laid — people are already wondering "so what's the solution?"

## 5. Launch Offer Structure

Since DEX AI is not a standalone product, the "offer" is the LYC Partners search engagement. But there are specific offers within the campaign that serve different funnel stages.

### Offer Architecture by Funnel Stage

**Top of funnel — no offer, just insight:**
- All Phase 1 content
- Whitepaper download (gated or ungated)
- Newsletter subscription
- No sales pitch. No call-to-action beyond "follow for more."

**Middle of funnel — low-risk, high-value offers:**
- **Complimentary single-candidate assessment:** Send us one of your finalists, we run them through DEX AI, you get the full report. No fee. No obligation. 48-hour turnaround.
  - Purpose: Proves the value in a concrete, low-risk way. Directly answers "is this real?"
  - Audience: Hiring managers, CHROs who are currently hiring
  - Where it appears: Middle-to-bottom of funnel content, webinar follow-ups, Phase 3 onwards

- **Complimentary team diagnostic (for leadership teams):** For founder/CEO audience — run your current executive team through a light diagnostic, get a team health snapshot.
  - Purpose: Opens the team-level conversation, leads to search engagements for critical roles
  - Audience: Founders, CEOs, leadership teams
  - Where it appears: Bottom-funnel content, Phase 3 onwards

**Bottom of funnel — the real offer:**
- **LYC Partners search engagement with DEX AI:** The core offer. Every engagement includes full DEX AI access — candidate assessment, market intelligence, team diagnostics, comparative analysis, full transparency dashboard.
  - Purpose: The actual revenue-generating offer
  - Audience: All B2B audiences who've moved through the funnel
  - Where it appears: "How we work" page, case studies, bottom-funnel content, proposal process

- **Existing-client talent intelligence retainer (Tier 2):** For clients who've done a search with us — ongoing talent market intelligence, leadership bench assessment, team diagnostics on retainer.
  - Purpose: Expansion revenue, deepens client relationship
  - Audience: Existing LYC Partners search clients
  - Where it appears: Post-engagement, client newsletter, Phase 4 content

### Launch-Specific Offers

**Complimentary assessment offer (available throughout campaign):**
- What it is: Send us two final candidates from your current search, we run them through DEX AI and give you the full comparative analysis
- Cost: Free — part of an introductory conversation with LYC Partners
- Why it works: Lowest possible risk for the prospect. Highest possible proof of value. If the assessment adds real value, the search engagement conversation is easy.
- Where it's promoted: Phase 3 onwards, in all bottom-funnel content, webinar follow-ups, LinkedIn DMs

**Founding insights partner offer (for Tier 2):**
- What it is: For existing clients who want to be first to use the full talent intelligence retainer (Tier 2), offered at a preferred rate during the launch period
- Cost: Discounted retainer for first 5 clients
- Why it works: Creates early Tier 2 revenue, gets client testimonials and case studies for future expansion
- Where it's promoted: Existing client outreach, Phase 4, client webinar

### Pricing Context

No public pricing is mentioned anywhere in the campaign. DEX AI doesn't have a price — it's included in LYC Partners search engagements. Search engagement fees follow LYC Partners' standard pricing (percentage of first-year compensation, retainer structure, etc.).

When people ask "how much does it cost?" the answer is: "DEX AI is included in every LYC Partners search engagement. The pricing is our standard search fee structure. We'd be happy to walk you through it on a call."

This is deliberate. Pricing a capability that's built into a service relationship is complex and context-dependent. We don't put a number on it because the comparison isn't "is this tool worth $X?" — it's "is LYC Partners with DEX AI worth the premium over another firm?"

## Phase 3 — Decision Gate

**Structural decisions to lock before Phase 4:**

1. **Launch model** — Capability Reveal Campaign (not a product launch). DEX AI discovered through content and experience, not announced on a launch date — lock?
2. **Timeline** — 12 weeks, 4 phases (Contradictions → Craft → Reveal → Proof), 3 weeks per phase — lock?
3. **Campaign phases** — Each phase deepens DEX AI visibility by one level. Phase 1: problem authority. Phase 2: methodology credibility. Phase 3: the reveal. Phase 4: proof and outcomes — lock?
4. **Content calendar** — One core insight per week, propagated across all five human voices + two brand voices. Week-by-week breakdown above — lock structure?
5. **Asset inventory** — Prioritized list of everything to build: whitepaper, 3 web pages, 70+ content pieces, 3-4 case studies, 2 webinars, email sequences — lock scope?
6. **Offer structure** — Complimentary assessment (mid-funnel proof) → LYC Partners search engagement (bottom-funnel offer) → Tier 2 intelligence retainer (expansion). No public pricing. No standalone DEX AI pricing — lock?

**Items that can be tweaked without relocking:**
- Specific posting cadence (exact days, exact number of posts per person)
- Individual content topics within each pillar / contradiction
- Exact timing of webinars and events
- Asset design details
- Specific offer wording

**Lock the structural decisions** → move to Phase 4 (Partnerships, Ecosystem Integration & KPI Framework).
**Revise any structural element** → specific feedback, then re-lock before proceeding.
