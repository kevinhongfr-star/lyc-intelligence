## Phase 5 Goal

Answer: **How does DEX AI generate value across the rest of LYC Partners — and how does the rest of LYC Partners make DEX AI stronger?**

DEX AI isn't just a search enhancement. It's a system that creates value at every layer of the business — and every layer feeds data and users back into DEX AI. This phase maps the specific pathways, signals, offers, and conversion math for each handoff direction.

## 1. The Cross-Business Map

LYC Partners has three distinct business surfaces. DEX AI connects all three — sometimes directly, sometimes through shared infrastructure, sometimes through handoff pathways.

### Handoff Direction Summary

| From → To | Direction | Mechanism | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| DEX AI → NEXUS | Candidate self-assessment → B2C product | Nudging candidates toward NEXUS for continued development | Medium |
| NEXUS → DEX AI | B2C executives → search or Tier 2 services | High-tier NEXUS users who show executive-level behavior | High |
| DEX AI → Coaching | Search candidates → coaching engagements | Candidates who get assessed and want development | Medium-High |
| Coaching → DEX AI | Coaching clients → assessment data + search pipeline | Coachees are candidates for future roles | Medium |

| LYC Brand → DEX AI | Content audience → search inquiries | DEX AI campaign → search engagements | Highest |

## 2. DEX AI → NEXUS (Candidate → B2C Self-Service)

### The Handoff

Every candidate who goes through DEX AI assessment (win or lose) gets a personal report. That report introduces them to the value of structured leadership assessment. NEXUS is where they can continue that journey on their own.

### Trigger Signals (Candidate Behavior)

| Signal | Meaning | Handoff Offer |
|---|---|---|
| Candidate completes assessment and reads the full report | Engaged, values the insight | "Want to track your development over time? NEXUS has ongoing self-assessment tools." |
| Candidate asks about development areas or how to improve | Proactive about growth | "We built NEXUS for exactly this — executive self-development between roles." |
| Placed candidate, 3 months post-placement | Settled into role, thinking about next level | "As you settle into the role, NEXUS can help you track your leadership growth." |
| Candidate says they're not actively looking but wants to stay sharp | Passive candidate, self-development oriented | "For between-role development, we have NEXUS — it's built on the same framework." |

### Offer Architecture

| Offer | What it is | Audience | Conversion Point |
|---|---|---|---|
| Complimentary NEXUS Executive tier (3 months) | Included for all placed candidates | Placed candidates | Post-placement, week 2-4 |
| Candidate referral discount | 30% off NEXUS for referred executives | Assessed candidates who refer peers | Assessment report footer |
| "Between roles" development program | Structured 6-week self-development journey on NEXUS | Candidates who weren't placed but are strong | Post-search follow-up email |

### Conversion Math (Rough Model)

| Step | Volume | Conversion | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Candidates assessed per year (all searches) | ~500 | 100% | 500 |
| Read full report | ~500 | 80% | 400 |
| Click NEXUS link in report/candidate portal | ~400 | 15% | 60 |
| Sign up for NEXUS | ~60 | 60% | 36 |
| Convert to paid tier | ~36 | 40% | 14-15 per year |

### What NOT to Do

- Don't sell NEXUS during the search process. It undermines the search relationship.
- Don't put NEXUS logos or promotion in client-facing materials. Client = search candidate is confusing.
- Don't share candidate data between DEX AI and NEXUS without explicit opt-in. Privacy first.

## 3. NEXUS → DEX AI (B2C → B2B Search / Tier 2)

### The Handoff

NEXUS has self-identifying executives using it for self-development. Some of them are decision-makers at their companies. Some of them are passive candidates open to opportunities. This is the B2C → B2B pipeline — high-intent, pre-qualified, already in the system.

### Two Handoff Paths

**Path A: Executive User → Search Engagement (or referral)**
- NEXUS users who are actively hiring or know someone who is
- They've experienced the assessment framework. They understand the value.
- They're much warmer leads than cold outreach.

**Path B: Executive User → Tier 2 Intelligence Retainer**
- NEXUS users at the executive tier who want team-level or company-level intelligence
- They've used the individual product. Now they want it for their org.
- Natural upsell from individual to team to enterprise.

### Trigger Signals (NEXUS User Behavior)

| Signal | Path | Meaning | Handoff Offer |
|---|---|---|---|
| User at Director+ level, inputs company size >500 employees | Both | Potential decision-maker | "We work with companies like yours on executive hiring. Want to talk?" |
| User takes team/bench assessment (even single-user mock) | B | Thinking about team-level insights | "We offer full team diagnostics through LYC Partners. Interested?" |
| User mentions hiring challenge in feedback or notes | A | Active hiring pain | "We specialize in exactly this kind of role. Happy to have a conversation." |
| User invites colleagues/peers to NEXUS | Both | Influencer/referrer type | "If you know companies that could use this at scale, let's talk." |
| User reaches Executive tier and engages heavily | Both | High LTV, high intent | "We offer enterprise-level intelligence through LYC Partners." |
| User searches for / asks about executive search or hiring firms | A | Active search intent | "LYC Partners is our executive search practice. We'd be happy to connect you." |

### Handoff Mechanism

**Automatic (in-product):**
- NEXUS tier upgrade page includes "Enterprise / Team — talk to LYC Partners" CTA at Executive tier
- In-app message when behavioral signals match (not pushy — contextual)
- Team / enterprise landing page on NEXUS that routes to LYC Partners

**Human (outreach):**
- NEXUS users showing enterprise signals get a personal email from Kevin or a partner
- "I noticed you've been using NEXUS — have you thought about what this would look like for your team?"
- Low volume, high touch. Only for qualified signals.

### Governance & Boundaries

- **Data wall:** NEXUS user data is not automatically visible to the DEX AI / search team. Users must opt in to being contacted about enterprise services.
- **Brand separation:** NEXUS is a consumer/self-serve experience. LYC Partners is a professional services firm. The handoff must feel like a natural next step, not a bait-and-switch.
- **No sales automation on the B2B side:** Every NEXUS → LYC handoff is personal and human. No drip campaigns, no automated sequences.

## 4. DEX AI ↔ Coaching Services

### DEX AI → Coaching

Every assessment generates development insights. Candidates who are placed (and even some who aren't) are prime candidates for executive coaching.

**Handoff triggers:**
- Placed candidate in new role — typical 90-day coaching need
- Candidate with strong development potential who wasn't the right fit for this role
- Client request for onboarding / integration support for new hire

**Offer:**
- 3- or 6-month executive coaching engagement
- Built on DEX AI assessment data — baseline measurement at start, progress tracking throughout
- Coaching + assessment combination = more concrete, more measurable than coaching alone

**Volume estimate:** 10-20% of placed candidates move to coaching engagements. Higher for senior roles (C-level, EVP).

### Coaching → DEX AI

Coaching clients get assessed and re-assessed. Their data feeds the model. Their success stories feed the content engine. Their referrals feed the search pipeline.

**What flows back:**
- Assessment data (before/after coaching = rich outcome data for model validation)
- Client success stories (coaching + DEX AI = better case studies)
- Referrals (coachees → search engagements for their companies)
- Coaching methodology insights (what works → improves assessment framework)

## 5. DEX AI ↔ Tier 2 Intelligence Retainer

This is the most direct handoff and the one with the clearest expansion path.

### DEX AI → Tier 2

Every search client experiences DEX AI through the engagement. After the engagement ends, Tier 2 is the natural continuation.

**Trigger points:**
- End of successful search engagement — "you've seen how this works for one role"
- Client asks about team-level or ongoing market intelligence
- Client has multiple roles to fill in a 12-month period
- Client expresses interest in talent strategy beyond a single hire

**Offer architecture (post-search):**
- **Tier 2 Lite:** Quarterly talent market briefings + annual team diagnostic (entry point)
- **Tier 2 Standard:** Monthly market intel + quarterly team assessments + bench tracking
- **Tier 2 Premium:** Dedicated intelligence support, real-time market monitoring, full team dashboard, custom projects

**Conversion math:**
| Step | Volume | Conversion | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| Search engagements per year | ~40-60 (current LYC scale) | 100% | ~50 |
| Clients who experience DEX AI fully | All going forward | 100% | ~50 |
| Qualified for Tier 2 (right size, right seniority) | ~50 | 50% | ~25 |
| Intro Tier 2 conversation offered | ~25 | 90% | ~22 |
| Convert to Tier 2 retainer | ~22 | 30-40% | 7-9 per year |

### Tier 2 → DEX AI (The Compounding Layer)

Tier 2 clients give us ongoing data that makes DEX AI better across everything:

- **More team data** = better team diagnostics = better search team fit assessments
- **More market data** = better market intel = better candidate benchmarking
- **More assessments over time** = better outcome validation = more accurate models
- **More enterprise use cases** = feature development pipeline for DEX AI

This is the strongest compounding loop in the whole ecosystem.

## 6. DEX AI ↔ LYC Brand & Content Engine

### DEX AI → Brand

DEX AI generates content fuel at a rate that pure thought leadership can't match:

- Assessment data → whitepapers, reports, data visualizations
- Client outcomes → case studies, success stories
- Candidate experiences → candidate perspective content
- Market intelligence → market trend content, benchmark reports
- Platform screenshots → visual content, methodology explainers

Every search engagement generates content fodder. The content engine never runs dry because the business is the content.

### Brand → DEX AI

The LYC brand and content engine is DEX AI's top-of-funnel:

- Thought leadership → people follow LYC → discover DEX AI → inquire about search
- Contradictions content → category creation → people want a better way → search inquiries
- Whitepapers and reports → email list → nurture → inquiries

### The Content Compounding Ratio

**1 search engagement = ~2-3 usable content pieces.**

At 50 engagements/year, that's 100-150 content pieces per year, all grounded in real work, real data, real outcomes. No invented content. No filler.

## 7. Handoff Governance & Principles

### Non-Negotiable Rules

1. **No data sharing without explicit opt-in.** Across every handoff.
2. **The handoff must feel natural, not salesy.** "Oh, you have that too? Perfect timing" — not "here's another thing we're selling."
3. **Each business unit has its own brand and its own relationship.** DEX AI connects them, but it doesn't merge them.
4. **Quality over quantity in handoffs.** A small number of high-quality, high-intent handoffs is worth far more than a large number of low-intent ones.
5. **The compounding effects are the real prize.** The handoffs themselves aren't the biggest revenue opportunity. The compounding is.

### Implementation Priority

| Priority | Handoff | Why | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| P0 | DEX AI → Tier 2 | Direct revenue, strongest compounding, shortest path | Launch with campaign |
| P1 | DEX AI → Brand content | Fuels top of funnel, zero marginal cost | Immediate |
| P1 | Brand → DEX AI (campaign) | The 12-week campaign itself | Campaign kickoff |
| P2 | DEX AI → Coaching | Good revenue, natural fit | 3-6 months post-launch |
| P2 | NEXUS → DEX AI | High-value pipeline, low acquisition cost | When NEXUS has Executive tier volume |
| P3 | DEX AI → NEXUS | Nice to have, not core | When NEXUS growth team picks it up |
| P3 | Coaching → DEX AI (data) | Compounding effect | As coaching practice grows |
