COMMS METHODOLOGY

5 Core Principles

From Sheet 9 of the Unified Strategy Tracker. Applied across the entire brand rollout.

01

Taxonomy Principle

Outcomes lead, methods follow, framework names recede. Every customer-facing asset must lead with what people get, not what we do or what we call our tools internally.

02

Decision Gates, Not Revisions

Each phase has a clear gate. Once a gate is passed and locked, we don't reopen it. This prevents endless circular revisions. Phase 1 (strategy) must be locked before Phase 2 (messaging) starts in earnest.

03

Anti-Positioning First

Define what we're NOT before we define what we are. The market needs to understand who we're displacing and why old models fail, before they can understand our new model.

04

One Engine, Many Voices

Shared positioning framework, multiple distinct voices. Each partner reinforces the same thesis from their own angle. The brand is the sum of all voices, not just one person's output.

05

The Product Never Speaks First

Intelligence and insight lead. Products and tools follow as evidence and delivery mechanisms. NEXUS is never the hero of the story — the insight is.

6 Common Pitfalls to Avoid

✗Skipping Phase 1 — jumping straight to content without positioning
✗Doing everything in one meeting — strategy + messaging + creative all at once
✗Revisiting locked decisions — circular revisions kill momentum
✗Leading with taxonomy — internal jargon before customer outcomes
✗Forgetting anti-positioning — no contrast = no differentiation
✗No North Star metric — you can't prove it worked if you didn't baseline

BRAND RULE — LOCKED

Internal vs External Language

The single most common brand failure. Every agent must pass this test: if a prospect reads your output and asks "what's the flywheel?" — you failed.

⚠ NEVER SAY EXTERNALLY
• flywheel
• 3-layer model / Layer 1 / 2 / 3
• maturity stack / stages / Stage 0–3
• signals / signal matching (client context)
• entry point / funnel (as positioning)
• endorsed brand / brand architecture
• taxonomy rule (cite externally)
• anti-positioning
✓ SAY INSTEAD
• describe the value or the programme
• Research & Intelligence / Programmes & Advisory / Executive Search
• describe where someone is in their understanding
• "what we're seeing" / market indicators / trends
• "start here" / "first step" / name the offer
• "NEXUS by LYC" (just state it)
• (the rule is applied, never mentioned)
• (internal strategy, never cited)

The Test

If a prospect, client, or LinkedIn reader would need to understand our internal operating model to understand the value — you failed.

They should understand: what we do, why it matters, and what's the next step. Nothing about our internal architecture.
Every agent has this rule in their brief.
Emily audits every deliverable before it goes out. Carl and Maria are the final gatekeepers — everything they publish is public-facing and must be 100% clean.
