# PRISM — Deep Canon
**professional branding**

---

## Model Explanation

PRISM is a B2C individual assessment that measures the strength and clarity of a person's professional brand across five dimensions. It is positioned in the P3 Strategic & Organizational Impact pillar as a related product — supporting individual leaders in building the personal brand and visibility they need for board-level and executive impact.

The model uses a self-report 30-question survey (6 per dimension) on a 5-point Likert scale. Outputs include a five-dimension radar profile, a personal brand archetype (one of ten), and development recommendations.

The ten archetypes sit on a 3×3 grid defined by two axes: **Visibility Level** (vertical: Low → Medium → High) and **Brand Consistency/Narrative Alignment** (horizontal: Low → Medium → High). This creates 9 cells with 9 archetypes, plus one additional archetype (The Blank Page) below the grid for very early-stage professionals.

**Important:** The two axes (Axis 1 and Axis 2) are structural grid lines, not archetypes. They define the map on which the ten real archetypes sit.

---

## Dimension Interrelationships

The five dimensions form a logical progression from internal to external:

**Internal → External cascade:**
1. **Brand Clarity** (internal self-definition) — do you know what you stand for?
2. **Identity Consistency** (cross-channel coherence) — does it hold together across contexts?
3. **Narrative Power** (story quality) — can you tell it compellingly?
4. **Market Legibility** (audience understanding) — does your audience get it?
5. **Visibility Level** (market presence) — how many people see it?

**Key insight:** Clarity and consistency are foundational. You can have high visibility with low clarity (The Monument / The Signal), but it's not a strong brand. The most powerful archetypes (The Authority) have high scores across all five dimensions.

## Archetype Detail

### The Authority
**Grid position:** High Visibility × High Consistency
**Dimension profile:** Strong across all five dimensions

The gold standard of professional branding. Clear, consistent, visible, and compelling. People in your market know who you are, what you stand for, and why it matters.

**Strengths:** Credibility, recognition, market position, pricing power
**Growth areas:** Complacency risk — need to continuously evolve without losing identity; intersectionality and expansion

---

### The Signal
**Grid position:** High Visibility × Medium Consistency
**Dimension profile:** High visibility, strong narrative power, moderate consistency

You're visible and people notice you, but your brand message is scattered. Different audiences get different impressions. You cut through the noise — but the signal isn't always clear.

**Strengths:** Visibility, reach, ability to get attention, charisma
**Growth areas:** Brand consistency, narrative focus, positioning clarity

---

### The Monument
**Grid position:** High Visibility × Low Consistency
**Dimension profile:** High visibility, low clarity and consistency

You're well-known — but for what? Your brand feels dated or inconsistent. People recognize the name but can't articulate the value. It's a monument: impressive from a distance, but closer inspection reveals cracks.

**Strengths:** Name recognition, track record, visibility
**Growth areas:** Brand refresh, narrative modernization, clarity of current positioning

---

### The Chameleon
**Grid position:** Medium Visibility × High Consistency
**Dimension profile:** High consistency, moderate visibility, strong adaptability

You fit in everywhere — different contexts, different audiences, different roles. People like you and you're reliable. But you lack a distinct signature. "Versatile" is a compliment; "unclassifiable" is a branding problem.

**Strengths:** Adaptability, cross-context fit, likability, broad applicability
**Growth areas:** Distinctiveness, memorable positioning, narrowing your focus

---

### The Amplifier
**Grid position:** Medium Visibility × Medium Consistency
**Dimension profile:** Solidly mid-range across all dimensions — upward trajectory

You're building momentum. Your brand is coming into focus and your visibility is growing. You're on the cusp of breaking through from "known in your circle" to "known in your market."

**Strengths:** Momentum, balanced profile, growth trajectory, potential
**Growth areas:** Doubling down on what works, narrowing focus, accelerating visibility

---

### The Operator
**Grid position:** Medium Visibility × Low Consistency
**Dimension profile:** Competent delivery, moderate visibility, low clarity and consistency

You're good at what you do and people in your organization know it. But your external brand doesn't match your actual capability. You're the behind-the-scenes operator — reliable, competent, and invisible to the broader market.

**Strengths:** Substance, delivery, results, reliability
**Growth areas:** External visibility, brand articulation, packaging your expertise

---

### The Ghost
**Grid position:** Low Visibility × High Consistency
**Dimension profile:** High clarity and consistency, very low visibility

You have a clear, authentic, well-defined personal brand — that almost no one sees. You know who you are and what you stand for, but you're not investing in making it visible. It's a high-quality product with zero distribution.

**Strengths:** Clarity, consistency, authenticity, substance
**Growth areas:** Visibility building, audience development, platform building

---

### The Mask
**Grid position:** Low Visibility × Medium Consistency
**Dimension profile:** Moderate consistency, low authenticity/clarity, low visibility

Your public brand feels carefully constructed — like you're wearing a professional mask. It looks right from a distance, but there's a gap between the person you present and the person you are. People can sense it even if they can't name it.

**Strengths:** Polish, professionalism, risk avoidance, control
**Growth areas:** Authenticity, personal voice, vulnerability, self-acceptance

---

### The Static
**Grid position:** Low Visibility × Low Consistency
**Dimension profile:** Low across the board — scattered signal, low visibility

Your brand is all static. Mixed messages across channels, no clear positioning, low visibility. It's not that you're bad at what you do — it's that no one can tell from your brand.

**Strengths:** Growth potential, blank canvas opportunity, no negative baggage
**Growth areas:** Everything — start with foundational clarity, then build systematically

---

### The Blank Page
**Position:** Below the grid — very early stage
**Dimension profile:** Very low across all dimensions — or no professional brand yet

You're at the very beginning of building a professional brand. Maybe you're early in your career, switching fields, or starting fresh after a long break. You have the advantage of a clean slate — no existing brand constraints.

**Strengths:** Fresh start, unlimited potential, no legacy to unlearn
**Growth areas:** Foundational brand definition — start with Brand Clarity before worrying about visibility

---

## Interpretation Framework

**Priority for development advice:**
1. **If Brand Clarity is lowest** → Start here. Everything else builds on clarity.
2. **If Visibility is lowest but Clarity is high** → Invest in distribution — you have a good product, just no audience.
3. **If Identity Consistency is lowest** → Audit channels and align messaging before increasing visibility.
4. **If Narrative Power is lowest** → Work on your story before scaling visibility.
5. **If Market Legibility is lowest** → Focus on positioning and audience understanding.

**Report structure:**
- Overall brand score + archetype
- Five-dimension radar chart
- Archetype deep dive (strengths, blind spots, development path)
- Three prioritized development actions
- Channel-specific recommendations

---

## Edge Cases

**All very high (The Authority):**
- Recommend diversification of brand expression and thought leadership expansion
- Guard against complacency

**All very low (The Static / Blank Page):**
- Start with Brand Clarity foundation — don't jump to visibility tactics
- Sequencing matters more than volume

**High Visibility, Low Everything Else (The Monument):**
- This is the most risky profile — high visibility with low substance creates vulnerability
- Recommend pulling back on visibility investment and redirecting to foundation building

**Mid-range across the board (The Amplifier):**
- Most common profile for mid-career professionals
- The key question is: where to focus next? Answer depends on career goals and target audience

---

## Banned Patterns — PRISM Specific

1. **Never call it "Professional Brand Legibility"** — legibility is one dimension (D2), not the whole product.
2. **Never count Axis 1/Axis 2 as archetypes** — they're the grid, not the people on it.
3. **Never present PRISM as a "personality test"** — it's a professional brand assessment, not a personality type.
4. **Never promise specific career outcomes** — the diagnostic identifies patterns, it doesn't guarantee results.
5. **Never use "architecture" language** — it's a brand assessment, not a "brand architecture" tool.
