## 1. Model Explanation

A 36-item executive capability assessment measuring performance across six dimensions: Strategic Thinking, Execution Excellence, Commercial Acumen, People Leadership, Adaptive Capacity, and AI Readiness. Leaders receive a composite executive readiness score, dimension-level verdicts, and a primary leadership archetype based on their highest-scoring capability dimensions.

The assessment measures 6 dimensions with 36 items on a 5-point Likert (1 = Strongly Disagree, 5 = Strongly Agree) scale. Each dimension is normalised to a 0–20 score, and the composite is expressed as a 0–100 percentile.

The archetype system classifies leaders into 10 archetypes based on Archetypes are determined by the leader's highest-scoring dimension pairs, classified by performance band (Advanced = 75+/100, Developing = 50–74, Emerging = <50). The Specialist is a single-dimension-dominant profile. The Seedling is a multi-low-dimension emerging profile..

- **High Strategic Thinking + High Execution Excellence:** Combined strength in strategic thinking and execution excellence creates a powerful positioning advantage.

- **Low Strategic Thinking + High Execution Excellence:** Execution excellence strength compensates for strategic thinking gaps, but only partially.

## 3. Archetype Detail

### A1 — The Architect
**Strength Dimensions:** Strategic Thinking, Adaptive Capacity  
**Band Level:** Advanced  

**Core Pattern:** Designs robust systems and can pivot them when context changes. Rare combination at senior executive level.

**Strengths:**
- Exceptional strategic thinking — direction, pattern recognition, multi-horizon perspective
- Strong adaptive capacity — pivots effectively, tolerates ambiguity, leads through change
- Can design for the long term while adapting to the present
- Rare combination of strategic depth and adaptive agility

**Growth Areas:**
- Over-engineering risk — may design systems that are too complex for execution
- Perfectionism slowing delivery — pursuing optimal at the cost of timely
- May underinvest in commercial urgency — strategy over speed-to-market
- Delegation discipline — may want to control the design too closely

**Development Focus:** Delegation discipline; 'good enough' calibration; commercial acumen deepening.

### A2 — The Catalyst
**Strength Dimensions:** Commercial Acumen, Adaptive Capacity  
**Band Level:** Advanced  

**Core Pattern:** Thrives in complexity and disruption; spots commercial opportunities others miss; acts fast.

**Strengths:**
- Strong commercial acumen — value creation, financial literacy, market intelligence
- Exceptional adaptive capacity — pivots fast, thrives in uncertainty
- Bold commercial moves in rapidly changing markets
- Sees opportunities others miss and acts on them quickly

**Growth Areas:**
- Impatience with process and governance — may move too fast for organisational capacity
- Risk of burning out teams — pace can exceed team absorption capacity
- May deprioritise people development for commercial results
- Can create quality issues if speed consistently overrides rigour

**Development Focus:** Sustainability mindset; team development investment; governance discipline.

### A3 — The Diplomat
**Strength Dimensions:** People Leadership, Strategic Thinking  
**Band Level:** Advanced  

**Core Pattern:** Builds coalitions, navigates politics, aligns diverse stakeholders around complex strategy.

**Strengths:**
- Strong strategic thinking — can articulate direction and connect it to broader context
- Exceptional people leadership — team architecture, succession, individualised development
- Builds coalitions across diverse stakeholder groups
- Maintains trust through strategic changes and difficult decisions

**Growth Areas:**
- May prioritise harmony over hard decisions — people-pleaser tendency
- Can be slow to confront underperformance — protecting relationship over standards
- Execution rigour may be weaker than relationship and strategic capability
- Commercial acumen may be underdeveloped — strong on people, weaker on P&L

**Development Focus:** Decisiveness under pressure; constructive confrontation capability; execution rigour.

### A4 — The Commander
**Strength Dimensions:** Execution Excellence, Commercial Acumen  
**Band Level:** Advanced  

**Core Pattern:** Delivers results through commercial discipline and operational rigour; highly credible in P&L conversations.

**Strengths:**
- Exceptional execution — consistent delivery, accountability architecture, resource discipline
- Strong commercial acumen — financial literacy, value creation understanding, commercial confidence
- Highly credible in P&L and results conversations
- Reliable — delivers what they commit to

**Growth Areas:**
- May neglect people development for short-term results — results-first mindset
- Can be directive at the expense of team capability-building — telling over developing
- Strategic thinking may be narrower — strong on execution, weaker on direction-setting
- Adaptive capacity may lag — strong in stable execution contexts, less so in disruptive ones

**Development Focus:** People leadership investment; long-term talent pipeline thinking; adaptive capacity.

### A5 — The Steward
**Strength Dimensions:** Adaptive Capacity, People Leadership  
**Band Level:** Developing  

**Core Pattern:** Guides teams through change with empathy and consistency; preserves team cohesion in uncertainty.

**Strengths:**
- Strong adaptive capacity — leads through change, tolerates ambiguity
- Good people leadership — maintains team cohesion during transitions
- Empathetic and consistent leadership through uncertainty
- Preserves morale and team stability in difficult contexts

**Growth Areas:**
- May lack strategic direction or commercial edge — change leadership without clear destination
- Decision conviction may be weaker — strong on process, less confident on direction
- Strategic thinking sharpness may need development
- Commercial acumen may be underdeveloped for senior roles

**Development Focus:** Strategic thinking sharpness; commercial acumen; decision conviction.

### A6 — The Strategist
**Strength Dimensions:** Strategic Thinking  
**Band Level:** Developing  

**Core Pattern:** Sees the big picture clearly; excellent at analysis and direction-setting.

**Strengths:**
- Strong strategic thinking — clear direction, pattern recognition, multi-horizon perspective
- Excellent analysis and direction-setting capability
- Can articulate complex strategy clearly
- Strong at identifying assumptions and strategic linkages

**Growth Areas:**
- Execution gaps — vision without delivery mechanism
- May rely on others for follow-through and implementation
- Team mobilisation capability may be weaker than analysis capability
- Accountability architecture may be underdeveloped

**Development Focus:** Execution Excellence; team mobilisation; accountability architecture.

### A7 — The Engine
**Strength Dimensions:** Execution Excellence  
**Band Level:** Developing  

**Core Pattern:** Reliable delivery machine; consistent output; operationally credible.

**Strengths:**
- Strong execution — reliable delivery, consistent output, operational credibility
- Good organisational design and accountability structures
- Operationally credible — knows how to get things done
- Resource discipline — makes tough allocation decisions

**Growth Areas:**
- May be executing the wrong strategy — strong delivery, weaker direction
- Tends to optimise the current path rather than question it
- Strategic thinking may be underdeveloped
- Commercial awareness and adaptive capacity may lag

**Development Focus:** Strategic thinking; commercial awareness; adaptive capacity.

### A8 — The Entrepreneur
**Strength Dimensions:** Commercial Acumen, Adaptive Capacity  
**Band Level:** Developing  

**Core Pattern:** Bold commercial moves; market-savvy; pivots fast when opportunity shifts.

**Strengths:**
- Strong commercial acumen — market-savvy, opportunity-spotting, commercial judgment
- Good adaptive capacity — pivots fast when opportunity shifts
- Bold commercial moves and risk-taking
- Entrepreneurial energy and initiative

**Growth Areas:**
- May lack organisational buy-in or people development capability
- Governance gaps — may move faster than organisational controls can handle
- People leadership may be underdeveloped — strong on opportunity, weaker on team building
- Execution discipline may be inconsistent — strong on start-up, weaker on scale-up

**Development Focus:** People leadership; execution discipline; stakeholder management.

### A9 — The Specialist
**Strength Dimensions:** One dimension dominant (>15 pts above all others)  
**Band Level:** Any band  

**Core Pattern:** Deep expertise in one executive capability domain; highly valued for that specific strength.

**Strengths:**
- Exceptional depth in one capability domain
- Highly valued for specific expertise
- Clear personal brand and value proposition
- Can serve as go-to expert in their area of strength

**Growth Areas:**
- Over-reliance on single strength — may have blind spots in 2–3 other dimensions
- Seniority ceiling may be limited by breadth of capability
- Risk of becoming a specialist leader rather than a general executive
- May struggle in roles requiring broader capability set

**Development Focus:** Broadening capability across secondary dimensions; delegation of dominant strength to create capacity.

### A10 — The Seedling
**Strength Dimensions:** Multiple low dimensions; average <50  
**Band Level:** Emerging  

**Core Pattern:** High development potential; early in the executive capability journey; typically high motivation.

**Strengths:**
- Often high motivation and learning appetite
- Early in journey — substantial growth runway
- Can develop rapidly with structured support
- Potential to grow into any archetype with the right development

**Growth Areas:**
- Overwhelm risk — pursuing development in too many areas simultaneously
- Wrong sequencing of priorities — trying to build everything at once
- Multiple capability gaps across executive dimensions
- May not yet know which strengths to build around

**Development Focus:** Focus on 1–2 highest-leverage dimensions only; structured mentoring; SHIFT-QUEST programme indicated.

## 4. Interpretation Framework

### Composite Score Interpretation

| Band | Score Range | Interpretation |
|------|-------------|----------------|
| Executive Ready | 80–100 | Strong capability across all six dimensions; operating at senior executive level. |
| Near-Ready | 60–79 | 1–2 capability gaps to address; strong foundation with targeted development needed. |
| Capability Building | 40–59 | Substantive development needed in 2–3 dimensions; structured programme recommended. |
| Early Executive | 20–39 | In transition to executive level; not yet operating at full capability. |
| Pre-Executive | 0–19 | Fundamental capability gaps; foundational development required. |

### Dimension-Level Interpretation

- **Strength (15–20):** Deployable capability; executive operates at this level consistently and under pressure
- **Developing (10–14.9):** Functional in stable conditions; gaps emerge under pressure or in unfamiliar APAC contexts
- **Gap (0–9.9):** Underdeveloped for the executive's current or target role; primary development priority
