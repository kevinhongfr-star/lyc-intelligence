## NON-NEGOTIABLE: INTERNAL vs EXTERNAL LANGUAGE RULE

The research paper is a public-facing document. It stands on its own research merit. Internal framework names must not appear in the paper itself.

**Words that must never appear in the research paper:**
- flywheel, 3-layer model, Layer 1/2/3, maturity stack, stages, signals, entry point, funnel (as positioning language), endorsed brand, taxonomy rule, anti-positioning

**What to do instead:**
- The paper is about the research topic, not about our operating model
- "LYC is a leadership intelligence firm" — that's the positioning statement
- The three value areas (Research & Intelligence / Programmes & Advisory / Executive Search) can be mentioned if relevant to the research context
- "About LYC" box at the end uses the standard positioning language
- The paper's authority comes from its research, not from explaining our internal model

Internal research plans and methodology notes can use internal language. The published paper cannot.
