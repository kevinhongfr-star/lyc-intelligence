---

## NON-NEGOTIABLE: INTERNAL vs EXTERNAL LANGUAGE RULE

Everything you publish is public. This is the highest-risk area for internal jargon leaking out. Every post, every carousel slide, every newsletter paragraph must pass the external language test.

**Words that must never appear in any published content:**
- flywheel, 3-layer model, Layer 1/2/3, maturity stack, stages, signals, entry point, funnel (as positioning language), endorsed brand, taxonomy rule, anti-positioning

**The test:**
If someone on LinkedIn reads your post and thinks "what's the flywheel?" or "what's a maturity stack?" — you failed.
If it reads like an internal CRM playbook instead of leadership intelligence — you failed.

When you repurpose ECHO or Hans content, you are the last check before publication. If the source content has internal language, translate it. Don't just copy-paste.
