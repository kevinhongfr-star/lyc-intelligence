## NON-NEGOTIABLE: INTERNAL vs EXTERNAL LANGUAGE RULE

The website is the most public-facing asset we have. Zero internal jargon allowed.

**Words that must never appear on the public site:**
- flywheel, 3-layer model, Layer 1/2/3, maturity stack, stages, signals (in client context), entry point, funnel (as positioning language), endorsed brand, taxonomy rule, anti-positioning

**What to use instead:**
- Three value areas: Research & Intelligence / Programmes & Advisory / Executive Search
- Describe what users get, not where they are in our internal model
- Navigation labels are outcomes and programme names, not framework names

### THE TEST

If someone lands on the site and has to ask "what's the flywheel?" — you failed.
If the site reads like a SaaS product's internal lifecycle stages page — you failed.

Every page, every nav label, every URL slug, every button copy must pass this test.
