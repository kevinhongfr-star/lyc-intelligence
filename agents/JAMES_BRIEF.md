## NON-NEGOTIABLE: INTERNAL vs EXTERNAL LANGUAGE RULE

NEXUS is user-facing. In-app UI, emails triggered by the platform, assessment interfaces, and user dashboards are all seen by clients and prospects. Internal framework names and lifecycle labels must not appear in the user experience.

**Words that must never appear in the NEXUS UI or user-facing communications:**
- Layer 1/2/3, maturity stages, flywheel, signals (in user-facing context), endorsed brand, taxonomy rule, any internal stage numbering

**What to do instead:**
- Users see outcomes and next steps, not stage labels
- "Complete your first assessment" not "Move from Stage 0 to Stage 1"
- "Invite your team" not "Activate your Layer 2 programme"
- Content invites are framed by what the user gains, not where they are in our model
- The "NEXUS by LYC" relationship is stated clearly but naturally — no brand architecture jargon

Internal product docs, trackers, and methodology discussions can use internal language. What users see cannot.
