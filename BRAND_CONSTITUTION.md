## 1. BRAND PROMISE & POSITIONING

### Brand Promise
**LYC — Leadership Intelligence**

Clean. Category-defining. No modifiers. Global scope with Asia depth as the proof point, not the boundary.

### Positioning Statement
LYC is a leadership intelligence firm. We diagnose gaps, design better teams, and deliver executive impact — across self-serve platforms, live programmes, and retained search.

### Core Differentiator
LYC is the only leadership intelligence firm that combines:
- A self-serve diagnostic platform (NEXUS)
- Live programmes and coaching (workshops, webinars, cohorts)
- Retained executive search and advisory

All built on the same IP stack. Three layers of engagement. One system.

### What We Are Not
- Not an executive search firm that also does advisory
- Not a consulting firm that also does recruiting
- Not a SaaS platform
- We are a leadership intelligence firm — intelligence is the category, search and advisory are how we deliver it at the highest level

### Anti-Positioning
**Primary antagonist: Traditional executive search firms**

Their frame: "We have the best network and the most rigorous process."

Our reframe: "Traditional search firms evaluate past performance. We evaluate future capability. We start with intelligence about what the role actually requires, then find the people who fit that future."

## 2. THE UNIFIED FLYWHEEL (Service Architecture)

### Layer 1 — Leadership Intelligence
Entry point. Data layer. Proof.
- NEXUS platform, thought leadership, webinars, Kevin's voice
- Customer question: "Where do I stand?"

### Layer 2 — Leadership Programmes
Skill-building. Revenue bridge.
- 27 workshop modules, 1:1 coaching, cohorts, council, webinars
- Customer question: "How do I get better?"

### Layer 3 — Executive Search & Leadership Advisory
Core. Retained. Highest margin.
- Retained search, leadership advisory, board advisory
- Customer question: "Who do I need, and how do we win?"

### The Reverse Loop (Layer 3 → Layer 1)
- Search candidates → NEXUS assessments
- Advisory clients → team diagnostics
- Programme participants → NEXUS access
- All clients → content + case studies

## 3. THE TAXONOMY RULE — NON-NEGOTIABLE

**Outcomes lead. Methods follow. Taxonomy recedes.**

### Level 1 — Primary (customer-facing, large/visible)
Outcome language only. No framework names. No code names.
- "Leadership Diagnostic" (not "SHIFT Assessment")
- "China Leadership Briefing" (not "NEXUS Report")
- "Executive Team Assessment" (not "MOSAIC Diagnostic")

### Level 2 — Secondary (product context, mid-page)
Functional description + "powered by [framework name]"
- "Leadership Diagnostic — powered by SHIFT"

### Level 3 — Tertiary (depth, methodology)
Framework names and methodology details. For people who want the full story.

**Check every piece of content:** Are we leading with outcomes? Are framework names only showing up where they add credibility, not where they create confusion?

## 4. MESSAGING ARCHITECTURE

### Hero Message (one sentence)
LYC gives you the intelligence to make better leadership decisions — for your team, your pipeline, and your next move.

### Three-Pillar Content Strategy
1. **The China Question** — China leadership, succession, geopolitical impact on talent
2. **The Future of the C-suite** — How executive roles are changing, what tomorrow's leaders need
3. **Intelligence-Driven Search** — Data-backed approach to finding and placing executives

### The Maturity Stack
B2C → B2B graduation in 4 stages, ~90 days:
- Stage 0: Casual (article/post reader)
- Stage 1: Engaged (assessment taker, newsletter subscriber)
- Stage 2: Committed (workshop attendee, programme participant)
- Stage 3: Partner (retained client, board advisory)

## 5. NEXUS — Endorsed Brand Model

NEXUS is "NEXUS by LYC" — endorsed, not sub-branded.
- NEXUS has its own visual identity but always signals LYC parentage
- NEXUS users know they're in the LYC ecosystem
- NEXUS feeds into LYC programmes and search (the graduation path)
- LYC never looks like a NEXUS feature — NEXUS is a LYC product
