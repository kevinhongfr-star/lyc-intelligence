## 1. The Old Flywheel (Pre-DEX AI)

Before DEX AI, LYC Partners' business flywheel is the standard professional services model:

**How it works:** Reputation → inquiries → engagements → satisfaction → more reputation → more inquiries. Growth comes from doing good work, getting referrals, and slowly expanding the brand.

**Limits of this model:**
- Growth is roughly linear with consultant capacity
- The learning curve is human-based — knowledge isn't systematically captured
- Each engagement is mostly independent — past engagements don't make future ones better
- The brand grows through anecdote, not data

This is how every boutique search firm operates. It works, but it caps out. Growth is slow. Scaling is hard. Margins are bounded by billable hours.

## 3. The Three-Layer Flywheel Architecture

The unified flywheel operates at three layers simultaneously. Each layer spins independently but feeds the others.

### Layer 1: The Intelligence Layer (DEX AI)

This is the center. This is what makes everything else accelerate.

**What spins it:**
- Every search engagement → assessment data → model improvement
- Every Tier 2 client → ongoing data → better benchmarks
- Every coaching engagement → before/after data → validation
- Every NEXUS user → self-assessment data → framework calibration (anonymized, aggregated)

**What it feeds:**
- Better search outcomes
- Better coaching outcomes
- Better Tier 2 intelligence
- Better content (data-backed, proof-driven)
- Better product development

**The metric:** **Assessment volume and model accuracy.** Every assessment makes the system marginally better.

### Layer 2: The Service Layer (Search + Coaching + Tier 2)

This is the revenue engine. It's powered by the intelligence layer.

**What spins it:**
- DEX AI makes search engagements better → higher close rate, higher client satisfaction, more referrals
- Search engagements generate Tier 2 leads → expansion revenue
- Search engagements generate coaching leads → expansion revenue
- Better outcomes → better reputation → more inbound

**What it feeds:**
- Data back to the intelligence layer
- Content for the brand layer
- User flow to NEXUS

**The metric:** **Revenue per consultant and engagement margin.**

### Layer 3: The Brand Layer (Content + Reputation)

This is the top-of-funnel engine. It's fueled by the other two layers.

**What spins it:**
- Data and stories from search engagements → whitepapers, posts, case studies
- Client and candidate testimonials → social proof
- Platform screenshots and demos → tangible proof of differentiation
- Contradictions and thought leadership → category creation

**What it feeds:**
- Inbound inquiries → search engagements
- NEXUS signups → B2C product users
- Audience growth → reach for all future campaigns

**The metric:** **Brand search volume and inbound inquiry rate.**

## 4. What Changes — and What Doesn't

### What Fundamentally Changes

1. **The firm's moat moves from "relationships + track record" to "relationships + track record + proprietary intelligence platform."** The first two are table stakes. The third is what makes LYC structurally different.

2. **Growth is no longer limited by consultant headcount.** The intelligence layer amplifies each consultant's capacity. More time on high-value work (client relationships, judgment, strategy), less on low-value work (sourcing, screening, report writing).

3. **Content is no longer a founder bottleneck.** The content engine is fueled by the work itself. Every engagement generates material.

4. **Client value becomes measurable.** Before, "better search" was subjective. With DEX AI, it's measurable: time-to-shortlist, first-round acceptance rate, quality-of-hire scores, client satisfaction.

5. **Pricing power increases.** When outcomes are provably better and the process is differentiated, "meets market rate" becomes "earns a premium."

### What Doesn't Change (and Mustn't)

1. **The primacy of human judgment.** DEX AI amplifies judgment, it doesn't replace it. The firm sells executive search delivered by senior partners, not software.

2. **The client relationship is still human.** Client conversations, strategy sessions, candidate presentations, negotiation — all still led by partners. DEX AI supports, it doesn't lead.

3. **LYC Partners is a search firm, not a tech company.** The platform exists to make search better.

4. **Quality over volume.** Better shortlists, not more shortlists. The flywheel accelerates quality.

5. **The brand is LYC Partners.** DEX AI is the engine, not the logo. Clients hire LYC Partners.

## 5. The New North Star (Unified)

### Primary North Star: **Client Outcome Score**

**Definition:** A composite metric that combines:
- Shortlist acceptance rate (60% weight) — did we get it right the first time?
- Time-to-placement (20% weight) — are we faster than the market?
- Client satisfaction (post-engagement rating) (20% weight) — does the client feel it was better?

**Why this is the primary North Star:**
- It captures the essence of what DEX AI is supposed to do — make search better
- It's directly measurable for every engagement
- If it's going up, everything else should be going up too

**Target:** Steady quarter-over-quarter improvement. No ceiling.

### Secondary North Stars (Supporting)

| Metric | Layer | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| Search Engagement Close Rate | Service + Brand | Is the message converting? |
| Revenue Per Consultant | Service | Is the intelligence layer making the team more productive? |
| Engagement Margin | Service | Is the platform creating operating leverage? |
| Brand Inbound Inquiry Rate | Brand | Is the content flywheel working? |
| Data Compounding Rate | Intelligence | Is the system actually getting smarter? |
| Cross-Sell Rate (Search → Tier 2) | Service expansion | Are clients moving up the value stack? |

### How You Know the Flywheel Is Working

All three of these moving up simultaneously:

1. **Client Outcome Score is going up** (the product/service is getting better)
2. **Inbound inquiry rate is going up** (the brand/content engine is working)
3. **Revenue per consultant is going up** (the business is getting leverage)

If one is up but the others aren't, something in the handoff between layers is broken.
