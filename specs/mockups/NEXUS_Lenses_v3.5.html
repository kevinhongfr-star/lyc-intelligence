Root Cause

What's actually happening

The leadership drift you're seeing isn't a people problem. It's a scaling problem that looks like a people problem. When your team was 25 people, decision-making was ambient — everyone knew what was happening because they were in the room. At 40, that room doesn't exist anymore.

The COO wants faster execution. The CFO wants more control. Both are right for their functions. The gap isn't between them — it's in the architecture that should connect their work.

Why it matters now

  • 018 weeks before director-level disengagement starts pushing high performers out.
  • 02Six cross-functional initiatives are stuck — each leaking time, money, and credibility.
  • 03Your board knows execution is the risk. They'll attribute any miss to leadership drift.
  • 04The cultural cost compounds: every week without a fix makes the eventual fix harder.
Action Plan
Wk 1-2
Critical

Clarify decision rights at the director layer

Define who decides what for cross-functional initiatives. Not another RACI — a simple decision map with three categories: decide, influence, inform. Focus on the 6 stuck initiatives first.

Wk 2-3
Critical

Realign the COO/CFO operating rhythm

Create a joint weekly 30-minute operational sync with a single agenda: what's blocking progress this week. One shared operational reality instead of two parallel views.

Wk 3-4
High

Unblock the 6 stalled initiatives

For each stuck initiative, assign a single accountable owner with a clear yes/no path to resolution. Kill the ones without a real sponsor. Better 8 moving than 14 in purgatory.

Wk 4-6
Medium

Director-level calibration conversations

Not performance reviews. Structured conversations about what's working, what's not, and what each director needs. Surface the concerns people aren't saying out loud.

Wk 6-8
Foundational

Update the operating model for 40+ people

The consensus-based model that got you to 25 won't get you to 50. Design a decision architecture that matches your current size — without losing the collaborative culture that got you here.
